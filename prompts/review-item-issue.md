## Issue review

Aim for one useful outcome: close the issue when current `main` or a merged PR demonstrably fixes it; route a bounded, high-confidence existing-behavior bug to a focused repair PR when no open PR owns the fix; otherwise name the concrete safety, evidence, or product-decision blocker. A read-only review need not run the bug when current source proves a small defect: describe that evidence accurately and let the implementation worker establish the failing regression. `AGENTS.md` is review input, not a source of findings: route its concerns about the issue, product direction, or maintainability through `risks`, `bestSolution`, `solutionAssessment`, or `workReason`.

`workReason` is the public **Next step**, not an instruction to the review host or implementation worker. State the concrete repair, missing evidence, or maintainer decision; do not tell the reader to reuse, queue, or dispatch a worker or lane, and do not infer that work has started from labels or previous review prose. For a source-proven repair, say what must be fixed and which failing regression must be established. Keep worker execution instructions in `workPrompt`.

- `bulkFiler.detected`: extra duplicate scrutiny, terse public prose, and no proof-nudge or automated fix-dispatch work. A likely duplicate is `duplicate_or_superseded`; bulk filing alone is no close reason.
- `autoImplementationCandidate: "strict_bug"`: `itemCategory` exactly `bug`, `reproductionConfidence` and `workConfidence` both `high`, all three `requires*` flags `false`, and `reproductionStatus` `reproduced`, or `source_reproducible` with `implementationComplexity: small` when current source establishes the defect and its narrow repair. Claim no live reproduction for source-proven work; the implementation worker reproduces or establishes a failing regression before opening a PR. Its `workPrompt` fixes the broken behavior, adds regression coverage, and stops before any feature, config, or policy change.
- `"vision_fit"`: high-confidence, VISION.md-aligned, `small`, `queue_fix_pr` work with `workConfidence: "high"`, a complete `workPrompt` with likely files and validation commands, no security or product-decision blocker, and no open linked PR.
- `visionFit`: `aligned` only for current or explicit future priorities without a roadmap-guardrail conflict; `unclear` when evidence is mixed or VISION.md is unavailable; `not_applicable` for non-product cleanup. `implementationComplexity: large` covers broad architecture, multi-package, migration, or cross-product work.
- Pull-request-only fields take their issue values: `reviewFindings: []`, `overallCorrectness: "not a patch"`, an honest low `overallConfidenceScore`; `realBehaviorProof` status and `evidenceKind` `"not_applicable"` with `needsContributorAction: false`; `securityReview.status: "not_applicable"` unless the report is security-sensitive (then `needs_attention` with typed concerns, or `cleared`); `nextStep` kind none with empty text, next-action guidance in `workReason`; `NA` for every `prRating` tier with empty `nextSteps`; `reviewMetrics: []`; `featureShowcase.status: "none"`; `telegramVisibleProof.status: "not_needed"`; empty `changeExample` strings; `mergeRiskLabels: []` and `mergeRiskOptions: []`; `productReview` kind, fixScope, and worthIt `not_applicable` with an empty `userProblem`; `testingReview.proofPath: "not_applicable"` with empty `lowValueTests` and `missingE2e`; `provenance: []`.
